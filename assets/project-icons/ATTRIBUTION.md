# Project technology icons

Brand SVGs come from [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0) under the included MIT license, and Obsidian/Notion from [Simple Icons 14.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/14.0.0) under the included CC0 license. Original file URLs are recorded in the app tech-catalog.js. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners and identify the tools entered by the user.

generic.svg and status-*.svg are original neutral code and glowing-dot symbols.

## Link icons

linkgithub.svg adds a light background to the bundled Devicon GitHub icon (MIT). youtube.svg uses the [Simple Icons 14.0.0 YouTube icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/14.0.0/icons/youtube.svg) (CC0) with red fill. website.svg is an original neutral globe symbol. Brand names and logos identify link destinations and do not imply endorsement.
